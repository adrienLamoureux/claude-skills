TL;DR

  • Agents scale; the person who reads, decides and checks does not. Human attention is the bottleneck now.
  • Four limits: reading volume, context switches, unknown terms, oversight fatigue. Fatigue costs the most.
  • Cut effort on format and search. Keep effort on understanding: full delegation lowers comprehension.
  • All nine options shipped on 2026-11-07: dev-kit 1.4.0, campaign-kit 0.4.0, docs-kit 1.1.0 and a new focus-kit 0.1.0.
  • focus-kit:Reader-first beat Concise: 173 vs 205 mean words, next move stated 3/3 vs 0/3.
  • Still unmeasured: the seeded-bug catch rate and words read per ticket.

The four limits of human attention

Each limit has its own cause, so each needs its own fix.

Reading volume

Long replies, plans and diffs overflow working memory. People skim and miss the risky line.

Context switches

Returning is fast; rebuilding the mental model is slow. Open loops leave attention residue.

Unknown terms

Experts forget what readers don't know. Jargon defined with jargon doesn't help.

Oversight fatigue

Checking AI output needs sustained, sceptical attention. Confident output slips past tired reviewers.

Design principles for human attention

Every option below applies one of these rules.

  1. Answer first Line 1 is the result or the decision. Detail lives in a file.
  2. Point, don't dump Show the 1-3 places that need human eyes.
  3. Small batches A diff you can hold in mind beats a big one reviewed tired.
  4. Close loops, few pings End each turn with "done" or "waiting on you for X"; notify on those only.
  5. Define on demand Gloss only the terms this reader doesn't know.
  6. Keep your model current Cut extraneous effort, not understanding.
  7. Gate by reversibility Stop for one-way doors; let reversible steps run.

When cognitive-load work fits a campaign

Researching and building were not campaigns. Tuning against a measured target is.

  1. Research and build One deliverable each, no rounds: done inline.
  2. Tune A campaign: halve the words read per ticket, keep the seeded-bug catch at 4 of 5.

Transcripts measure what you read and decide. Only you can rate fatigue.

Pitfalls

Less text is not always less load. Short summaries can hide risk.

Trust by brevity

Crisp summaries invite trust, and trust lowers checking. Keep Unverified and Residual risks on the card.

Delegating understanding

Pure delegators learned least. Keep the explain path open.

Output-style traps

Set keep-coding-instructions: true. Restart after editing a style.

Ping storms

Each notification is an interruption. Notify only when you must act.

Glossary

The terms this page uses, in one line each.

Extraneous load
Effort on format, search and noise. Cut it.
Germane load
Effort that builds understanding. Keep it.
Attention residue
Attention stuck on a task you left unfinished.
Oversight tax
Extra effort from checking AI output; "brain fry" is its extreme.
Automation complacency
Checking drops as the tool proves reliable.
Progressive disclosure
Essentials first, detail on request.
Decision card
Five lines at every gate: Decide, Recommend, If wrong, Undo cost, On "ok".
