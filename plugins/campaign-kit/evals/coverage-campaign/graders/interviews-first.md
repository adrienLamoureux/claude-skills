Before any test is written, the response interviews the user (asks, or lists the questions it
needs answered) to pin down: measurable done criteria (a coverage target and how it is
measured, plus a guard on CI time), the parallel lanes or workstreams, guardrails on
outward-facing actions such as pushing or opening PRs, and a round budget or checkpoint
cadence. It explains or implies that work then proceeds in rounds that are measured and
judged. It does not start writing tests immediately.
